What the test finds

The Problem Shows on the Faucet. The Test Shows the Cause.

Pittsburgh water is moderately hard, and it changes by neighborhood and source. The same symptom can have two causes, so the test comes first.

Hard water and scale

White crust on faucets, spotty dishes, dry skin after a shower.

Usually a water softener or refiner

Bad taste or smell

Chlorine taste, or a rotten-egg smell from the hot water.

Usually carbon filtration or a drinking water system

Rust and orange stains

Stains in toilets, tubs and laundry that keep coming back.

Often iron. Filtration sized to the result. confirm iron filters

What you can't see

PFAS, lead and chloramine don't change how water looks or tastes.

Reverse osmosis at the kitchen tap. UV for bacteria.
