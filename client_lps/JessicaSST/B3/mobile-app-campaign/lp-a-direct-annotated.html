What changed in the form, and why each change matters

  • The budget dropdown is gone entirely. On the current page it's required, and the first option is "Under $25,000." That asks a stranger to disclose their number before you've given them anything, and it tells them the conversation is about your revenue rather than their problem. It's the single most anxious field on the existing form.
  • The CAPTCHA is gone. It rejects real people at a measurable rate. Spam gets handled invisibly on the back end instead, where the visitor never has to prove anything.
  • Four fields, and four is the ceiling. Published form data puts the steep drop-off between four and seven fields.
  • The phone number stays right next to the form. A share of buyers at this deal size will never fill in a form, and a phone call is a better lead anyway. Making them choose costs you the ones who'd have called.
  • "If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you that instead" is doing more work than it looks like. The fear stopping a form fill at this price is not the price — it's being pulled into a sales process. Naming that fear removes it.

Everything here is measurable once it's live. We'll see who starts the form, who finishes it, and who calls instead, as separate numbers.
