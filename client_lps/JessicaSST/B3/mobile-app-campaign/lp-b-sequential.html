Questions we get asked

How can you quote a fixed price before you've built it? +

Because we scope first. We work through the users, workflows, integrations, and requirements before quoting anything. That work is what makes the number reliable — and it's why our proposal takes longer to produce than a rate card.

What if we change what we want mid-project? +

Real changes get priced as a change — openly, before we do the work. What you don't get is a surprise invoice because the original estimate was optimistic. Our misjudgment is on us.

Who actually writes the code? +

Our own engineers, in Pittsburgh. Nothing is subcontracted offshore. With 90% retention over the past decade, the people who start your project are generally the people who finish it.

Do we have to replace the systems we already run? +

Usually not. Most of our work extends what's already there — connecting ERP, CRM, GIS, accounting systems, databases, and legacy software rather than ripping them out.

What does a project cost? +

It depends entirely on scope, integrations, and security requirements — which is why we scope before quoting rather than publishing a range that wouldn't apply to you. You'll have a real number in writing before you commit to anything.
