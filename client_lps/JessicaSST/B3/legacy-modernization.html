FAQ

Common Questions About Legacy Modernization

Can you modernize software you didn't build?

Yes — we do it regularly. We take over systems built by other firms, retired vendors, and in-house teams that have moved on. We reverse-engineer what's there, document it, and build on top of it.

What if we don't have the source code?

We can work with compiled applications, existing databases, and screen-level interactions to understand your system. We've modernized software where the original source code was lost years ago.

How much does legacy modernization cost?

Most projects fall in the $50K–$250K range, depending on complexity and scope. We provide a fixed-fee quote after our free assessment — so you know the exact cost before committing.

Do you support the system after launch?

Every project includes a one-year warranty at no additional cost. After that, we offer ongoing support agreements tailored to your needs — from monitoring to feature development.

Can you do this in phases?

Yes — and most of our clients prefer it that way. Phased delivery means you see real results early, minimize disruption, and can adjust priorities as you go. We build it so each phase delivers standalone value.
